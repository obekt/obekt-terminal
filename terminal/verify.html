PROOF OF TRADES

Every day at 00:03 UTC, an isolated script on our server reads the exchange's own account equity and open positions straight from OKX, signs that snapshot with an ed25519 key, and appends it to a hash chain — each line seals the previous one. The current chain head is additionally committed to our public GitHub repo, so the head is timestamped by a third party (GitHub's commit history). Editing history later would break every later signature, and re-signing the whole chain would produce a head that no longer matches the GitHub anchor.

WHAT THIS PROVES · WHAT IT DOES NOT

  • Proves: the equity numbers below were reported by the exchange and signed by the key holder, in order, without edits or gaps, on the stated dates.
  • Does not prove: that the strategy is any good, that returns will continue, or that the key holder won't quit tomorrow. Bring your own skepticism.
